# REQUIRED STORY FLOW — all existing and future Ayla’s Puzzles games (18 Nov 2026)
This supersedes earlier directions for inline narrative/story panels. Each new game/puzzle instance presents its current story and illustration in a separate intro composition with a clear Begin/Play action. After beginning, gameplay contains no persistent intro story, narrative paragraph, story card/window, or leftover story panel. Preserve scene artwork and characters, game title, essential task/instructions, mathematical facts and persistent clues; these are not intro stories. Reclaim the freed space for proportionally larger play elements or unobstructed existing illustration while preserving each scene’s approved design. Material redesigns require renders approved before implementation.
Keep a small scene-native Story button during play. It opens the same current instance’s story above gameplay in a dismissible accessible overlay, with a visible Close control, Escape, focus management/return, and narration cancellation. Opening/closing must preserve the board, input, validation state and seed. New numbers/board retains the narrative and stays in play. Next starts a fresh instance with an intro; Previous/resume and rotation preserve prior phase and work. Reuse existing shared state, narration and story systems. Preserve ages, settings and independent releases. Test intro/Begin/reopen/close, New, Next, Previous/resume, rotation, keyboard, touch and reduced motion at all supported sizes. Implementation is being reviewed in isolated story-intro drafts; this policy is not evidence of completion or publication. Never publish this round without Jordan’s explicit approval of each game/version.

The dashboard release registry identifies current independent production projects. Use those sources, not stale game-list preview links. Preserve concurrent loader, audio, control-layout and game-specific edits. Before combining approved changes, reconcile shared AGENTS.md, route/session state, scoped layouts and commons-launch.ts narrowly; do not replace shared files wholesale. Approval is per game/version.
